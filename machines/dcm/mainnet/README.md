# DCM Ethereum integration coverage

DCM gains **17 supply integrations** and **Merkl reward harvesting** on Ethereum, bringing the configuration to **29 positions** (11 retained pre-existing; 2 pre-existing Aave V4 supplies removed). The PR covers Aave V3/V4 stablecoin supplies, Steakhouse and Sentora vaults, Pendle Ecosystem USDC, RockawayX f(x) Protocol Ecosystem USDC and PST/PYUSD lending.

**$5m Aave cutoff:** removed 36 Aave positions whose individual reserve had less than $5 million directly supplied at the snapshot. The remaining 6 Aave positions meet both that threshold and the APR cutoff below. For V4 the cutoff uses the Spoke reserve’s direct supplied balance, even when its shared Hub holds more liquidity.

**3% APR cutoff:** removed 10 supply positions using the 2026-09-08 yield snapshot (on-chain block [25932933](https://etherscan.io/block/25932933), 13:25:59 UTC): Aave V3 Core GHO (0.00%), USDe (1.61%), USDG (1.67%); Prime/Lido GHO (2.87%); Horizon GHO (1.57%) and RLUSD (1.78%); Aave V4 Main USDG (2.10%) and frxUSD (1.91%); Steakhouse USDT V1 (2.96%) and Steakhouse High Yield Instant AUSD (2.45%). The last two Aave V4 entries were pre-existing. Vault comparisons include rewards and convert the official app's base APY to an APR equivalent using ln(1 + base APY); Steakhouse USDT's 3.01% APY corresponds to 2.96% APR. Aave and direct Morpho market rates use base supply APR, excluding external incentives. This is a snapshot filter, not an ongoing rate guarantee. PRIME collateral, borrowing, conversion and Merkl claim actions have no comparable standalone earned APR and remain available. Rate definitions: [Morpho](https://docs.morpho.org/developers/earn/tutorials/get-data/), [Aave V4 Hub](https://github.com/aave/aave-v4/blob/main/src/hub/libraries/AssetLogic.sol).

**TVL snapshot:** Ethereum block [25932632](https://etherscan.io/block/25932632), **2026-09-08 12:25:35 UTC**. Values mean gross supplied assets, including borrowed-out funds: Aave reserve supply, Morpho vault totalAssets, or total loan assets supplied to a Morpho market. Prices come from Aave oracles, with AUSD priced through the Makina registry in USDC terms. Conversion-only entries have no standalone pool TVL.

**How to read TVL:** each Aave V3 row shows its stablecoin reserve supply and the entire instance’s supply across all assets. Each V4 row shows direct Spoke reserve supply and the shared Hub asset-pool supply. Morpho vault rows show the vault’s total underlying assets in USD; Morpho market, collateral and debt rows show the linked market’s total supplied loan assets. Broader market/Hub values and repeated Morpho market values must not be summed across rows. All figures use the same snapshot above. “New” means added by this PR, not a new protocol deployment.

**Morpho vaults below $5m:** marked ⚠ in the table. Two vaults remain included: Steakhouse High Yield USDT and Grove x Steakhouse USDC.

**Pool database metadata (queried 2026-09-08):** `pool_db ticker(s)` is `supply_tokens` from `medici-354713.pool_db.meta`. ✅ Matched means the configured position ID exactly equals the row’s `makina_id`; its link opens the database dashboard. IDX and INST are the raw `has_position_index` and `has_instructions` flags. ❌ means the database reports false, not that this PR failed compilation. Two PRIME conversion routes and the Merkl harvest entry are config-only. Market links use `meta.link` when populated, otherwise the verified vault, market or contract link.
